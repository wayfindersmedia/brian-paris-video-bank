02Hook rules

6 rules for every clip

  • Your first sentence is the hook. No "Hey guys", no "What I see so often...". Start on the point. The first 2 seconds decide if he keeps watching.
  • Name a specific moment, not a concept. "You yelled at your kids again" stops the scroll. "How do you handle emotions?" doesn't.
  • Talk to one man. Say "you", like you're talking to one client across the table.
  • One idea per clip. If you catch yourself saying "and another thing", stop. That's the next video.
  • Use simple words. If a guy at the gym wouldn't say it, swap it (see the language table below).
  • Mess up a line? Pause for 2 seconds and restart the sentence. Keep recording, we'll cut it.

Why a DM, not a comment

  • It's private. A man whose wife, ex or family might see his comments won't type anything public about his marriage. A DM stays between him and you.

Please skip: asking for public comments about their situation, "comment READY", "comment Men", events or other offers at the end of these videos. One CTA, every time. And no promises of outcomes ("save your marriage", "win custody").
